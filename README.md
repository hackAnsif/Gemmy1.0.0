# Gemmy – Smart Reminder

**Gemmy** is a lightweight, fast, 100% offline Android reminder application built with Jetpack Compose, Room database, and Material Design 3.

## Features

- **Fast Manual Reminder Creation**: Enter a reminder description, pick date and time, toggle alarm notification, and save in seconds.
- **Offline Smart Date & Time Parsing**: Automatically detects natural-language dates and times (e.g., `"Tomorrow 12 PM submit assignment"`, `"November 25 at 8 AM study physics"`, `"Today 6 PM complete project"`) with zero external AI APIs or internet connection.
- **Organized Home Screen**: View Overdue, Today's, Upcoming, and Completed reminders with instant search and filter chips.
- **Reliable Native Notifications**: High-priority reminder alerts, inline **Mark Done** & **Snooze** actions, duplicate prevention, and automatic restoration after device restart.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Local Room persistence with zero network permissions or cloud tracking.
- **Home Screen Widget & Adaptive Icon**: Quick glance at your next upcoming reminder and one-tap reminder creation.
